Our Story

Grinding Goodness Since 2007

Anandi Atta Chakki was born from a simple belief — every family deserves fresh, pure, chemical-free flour. What began as a small chakki service in Satara has grown into a full-fledged showroom offering premium flour mill machines to homes and businesses across Maharashtra.

We don't just sell machines — we help people bring the tradition of fresh, home-ground atta back into their kitchens. Every machine is tested, every customer is guided personally, and every purchase comes with genuine after-sales care.

Serving 5,000+ happy customers across Satara, Karad, and beyond — since 2007.
